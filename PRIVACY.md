# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 27 October 2026_

Breathe is a breathing app made by geno-whirl. This policy covers the Breathe app for Android, however it was installed (Google Play, F-Droid or a downloaded APK).

## What Breathe collects

Nothing. Breathe has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it does not ask for the internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

## What Breathe stores on your phone

- Your breathing pattern, presets and settings.
- Your session history: for each session of 10 seconds or more, its date and time, preset, pattern, breathing time, number of cycles and whether it ran to the end.

This stays on your phone. If Android's own backup is turned on, Android may include it in your device backup, which is handled by your phone's backup service, not by Breathe. When you export your history as a CSV file, the file goes where you choose to save it.

Uninstalling Breathe, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes all of it.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** and **foreground service**: to show the running session, with Pause and Stop, while the screen is off.
- **Vibration**: for the vibration cues.
- **Wake lock**: to keep the cues on time while the phone is locked.

Spoken cues use your phone's text-to-speech engine. Breathe only passes it the words "Breathe in", "Breathe out" and "Hold".

## Children

Breathe collects no data from anyone, children included.
