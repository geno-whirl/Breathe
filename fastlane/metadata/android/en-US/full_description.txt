A calm breathing companion built around four stages: inhale, hold, exhale, hold. It keeps guiding you with sound and vibration while the phone is locked, and its home-screen widget does everything the app does.

YOUR PATTERN, YOUR WAY
• Set each stage as a whole number; use 0 to skip a stage.
• Time it in seconds per stage, in counts of a length you choose, by total cycle length, or by breaths per minute.
• Presets for box breathing, 4-7-8, 1-4-2, coherent breathing (5.5 per minute) and more. Save your own, and update, rename or delete them.
• Sessions can be endless, a number of minutes, or a number of cycles.
• Tap the animation to count down in seconds or in counts.

GUIDED BY SOUND
• Singing bowl, chime, soft tone, marimba or spoken cues, all synthesised on the device.
• Every cue is tuned to the same scale, so they sound good together.
• Volume, optional hold cues and an optional soft tick each second.
• A closing chord and bell when a timed session ends.

GUIDED BY TOUCH
• Pulses: a rising double tap to breathe in, a falling one to breathe out.
• Wave: soft taps that strengthen through the inhale and fade through the exhale, so you can follow with your eyes closed.
• Uses your phone's built-in haptic effects when available, with adjustable strength.

WORKS WITH THE SCREEN OFF
Sessions keep running when the phone is locked. The notification shows the current stage and a countdown, with Pause and Stop buttons on the lock screen.

A WIDGET THAT DOES EVERYTHING
Start, pause and stop, follow the current stage on a progress bar, switch presets and toggle sound or vibration, all from the home screen.

YOUR HISTORY
Every session is recorded with its date, pattern, breathing time and cycles. See your totals for the last 7 days and all time, and export everything as a CSV file.

BEAUTIFUL AND PRIVATE
A glowing orb, a box or a ring animation in light and dark themes. No ads, no tracking, no account and no internet permission: your data never leaves your phone.
